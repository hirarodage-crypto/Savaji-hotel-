About Savaji Hotel

“Welcome to Savaji Hotel, where traditional Indian flavours meet freshly prepared food. We serve delicious non-vegetarian dishes and quick snacks with authentic spices and homestyle taste.”

Originating from the legendary weaving communities of Maharashtra and Northern Karnataka, Savaji food is distinguished by its fiery red tarri, hand-roasted coriander and black peppercorn blends, and slow-simmered tender meats that melt right off the bone. We refuse artificial colorings and shortcuts: every dish is prepared fresh upon order to deliver uncompromised flavour and warmth.
